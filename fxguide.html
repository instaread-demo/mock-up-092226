Written and directed by David Robert Mitchell, The End of Oak Street stars Anne Hathaway and Ewan McGregor in a story of suburban life turned upside down and back in time. When a mysterious event transports their neighbourhood somewhere unknown, and the Platt family must pull together to survive.

 

Join the team for the latest episode as the team digs deep into how the film was made, debates what worked and what didn’t, and puts the filmmaking choices under the microscope. Listen in and see where you ‘end up’ in the debate.
