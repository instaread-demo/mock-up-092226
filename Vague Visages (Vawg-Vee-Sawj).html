Soundtracks of Cinema: ’72 Hours’

By Q.V. Hough on September 18, 2026

Listen to audio version of this article

Instaread

The 72 Hours soundtrack includes music by Pitbull, LL Cool J and Daddy Yankee. This info article contains spoilers and song details for Tim Story’s 2026 Netflix movie. Check out Vague Visages’ Soundtracks of Cinema category for more soundtrack song listings.

72 Hours stars Kevin Hart as Joe, a marketing executive. Marcello Hernández co-stars as Nick, a young man who organizes a bachelor party. The storyline follows the main protagonists as they find trouble in Miami. Composer Christopher Lennertz (The Boys) scored the 105-minute Netflix film; music supervisor Kier Lehman (Project Hail Mary) selected the featured needle-drops. Here’s every song in 72 Hours.

72 Hours Soundtrack: Every Song in the 2026 Netflix Movie

  • “The Largest” by BigXthePlug (00:00:00): The 72 Hours soundtrack song scores the opening title sequence. A New York City-themed montage begins. Joe drives a vehicle and receives a phone call from his girlfriend, Jennifer (Teyana Taylor).
  • “Come Down to Miami” by Willie Colón, Celia Cruz (00:10:00): Joe finishes a conversation with Jennifer. The 72 Hours music continues during a “Friday” sequence. Joe meets with his bachelor party crew in Miami.
  • “Smells Like Money” by Yung Gravy (00:12:00): Joe and company check out their Miami rental. Freshman (Kam Patterson) enters the back yard area. He says, “Mason, look! It’s a like a forest out here! With no trees.”
  • “The Anthem” by Pitbull feat. Lil Jon (00:15:00): Joe and Nick continues a kitchen conversation. The 72 Hours soundtrack song continues during a transition sequence. Joe reacts to his dirty suitcase.
  • “Tumba Palo Cucuyé” by Afro Cuban All Stars (00:17:00): Joe tries on an outfit. He says, “I don’t know, guys. I feel ridiculous, but this is all I could find on such short notice.” The track fades during a transition scene at a liquor store.
  • “Silver Tooth.” by Silver Tooth. (00:22:00): Joe and company enjoy themselves in a limousine. They drink alcohol and dance together. Joe says, “Freshman, put some old school on.”
  • “Headsprung” by LL Cool J (00:23:00): A limousine sequence continues. The 72 Hours soundtrack song plays from a speaker system. Joe dances to the music.
  • “No Cap” by Disclosure, Anderson .Paak (00:25:00): Joe and company run from Officer Willard (Mike Epps) and Officer Cisneros (Sophia Del Rio). A chase sequence begins. The 72 Hours music plays again at 01:28:00.
  • “Do It to It” by ACRAZE (00:30:00): A marina sequence begins. Joe walks on a dock and talks on his telephone. Freshman stares at a group of women and says, “Oh, shit. Titties.”
  • “Dientes” by J Balvin (00:31:00): The 72 Hours soundtrack song scores a yacht sequence. Mason (Mason Gooding) says, “Oh! Joe, you the GOAT, man.” He wears a brown and white shirt.
  • “Gasolina” by Daddy Yankee (00:41:00): A yacht sequence continues. Joe imitates Tony Montana (Al Pacino) from the 1983 film Scarface. The 72 Hours music is featured again at 00:42:00.
  • “Kimbara” by Barry Can’t Swim (00:44:00): Joe and company eat at a restaurant. Mason looks at a photo book. He thanks Nick for the gift.
  • “Can’t Touch This (R3HAB Remix)” by Bia & R3hab (00:47:00): The 72 Hours soundtrack song scores a night club sequence. Joe talks to Hunter (Ben Marshall) about talking to women. Mason, Nick and Freshman listen to the drunken conversation.
  • “Move Ya Body” by Nina Sky featuring Steven Beckford (00:48:00): A night club sequence continues. Hunter points at a member of a bachelorette party named Scarlett (Elysée Sanvillé). Dallas (Geena Quintos) flirts with Joe.
  • “Rack” by callmestevieray (00:53:00): Joe talks to Mason in a limousine. The 72 Hours music plays from the speaker system. Joe reveals that Jennifer is moving out of their home.
  • “(I Got) So Much Trouble in My Mind” by Joe Quarterman (as Sir Joe Quarterman) & Free Soul (00:57:00): The 72 Hours soundtrack song scores a beach sequence. Hunter complains about being trapped on an island. He says, “We’re gonna have to resort to cannibalism.”
  • “Victory Lap” by Fred Again…, Skepta, PlaqueBoyMax (01:10:00): Joe confronts Dallas’ bachelorette crew in a pink limousine. He gets hit with various hand bags. The track continues during a fight sequence.
  • “Everybody Mad” by O.T. Genasis (01:19:00): Joe imitates Tony Montana from Scarface. A transition sequence begins. Joe and company meet with a drug lord (Andy Garcia).
  • “Shake Senora” by Pitbull featuring T-Pain and Sean Paul (01:31:00): A “3 Weeks Later” sequence begins. Mason and his wife, Emily (Amenah), celebrate at their wedding reception. The 72 Hours music continues as Joe talks to Nick.
