Why Tennessee Football Went For Two Early In Its Win Over Kennesaw State

  • By Ryan Sylvia
  • - Sep 20, 2026

With less than a minute to go in the first quarter, Tennessee football quarterback Faizon Brandon found Braylon Staley for a 13-yard passing touchdown on Saturday night. This gave the Vols a 13-3 lead over Kennesaw State with the extra point to come.

However, instead of knocking through a kick for one additional point, UT went to a swinging gate look with its line all set up to the left. Long snapper Bennett Brady snapped the ball with an underhand toss to tight end Luca Wolf, who used the blockers to punch it in for a two-point conversion.

Theories quickly arose about why Tennessee decided to get into this look and go for two. Was there bad blood between Vols head coach Josh Heupel and his former assistant coach turned Kennesaw State head coach Jerry Mack? Was this in retaliation for a late hit on Staley earlier that drive?

Heupel answered the question during his postgame press conference. When asked about the decision, he simply responded by saying the choice is based on the look the defense presents.

There’s no reason to think Heupel is lying, either. Tennessee has lined up like this multiple times in prior games this season, ultimately not getting the intended look and adjusting for a regular extra point kick. This time, the Owls didn’t match the numbers, though, and UT took advantage.

Heupel also has had nothing but great things to say about his former assistant, Mack. He complimented his team and the staff that he has around him at Kennesaw State during his press conference leading into the game. He echoed the same sentiment afterward.

“Good to see him,” Heupel said. “He has got a good football team. They have done a good job there last year. When they get into conference play, they are going to have a chance.”

Next, Tennessee begins its own conference play. The Vols will host No. 1 Texas on Saturday at noon ET. The game will air on ABC.
