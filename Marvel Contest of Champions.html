The Ultimate Doom Sweepstakes

Greetings Summoners!

Do you dare to face Doom? A few lucky Summoners will do just that with a chance to attend The Premiere of Avengers: Doomsday in Los Angeles! From September 16th through December 9th, collect Ultimate Doom Raffle Tickets in the game, and sign up below to enter The Ultimate Doom Sweepstakes and your chance at glory!

Enter For A Chance To Win

  • 1 VIP Avengers: Doomsday LA Premiere Experience (includes Flights and Hotel).
  • 4x Exclusive El Capitan Hollywood Fan Screenings (includes Flights and Hotel).
  • iPhone 16 & Google Pixel devices.
  • App Store & Google Gift Cards

As well as In-Game Prizes such as:

  • 450x, 750x, 1500x, 20,000x Units.
  • 5-7-Star* versions of Yelena Belova, Namor, or Thing.
  • And 6-Star Doctor Doom

*Champion rarity will be determined by Player Progression. See the Official Rules for full details.

Draws take place on October 8th, November 5th, and December 10th, with the Grand Prize Draw for the Doomsday World Premiere and El Capitan Fan Events on November 2nd.

Physical and In-Person prizes are only available in certain regions. See Official Rules for details.

How do You Enter?

You must create A Kabam ID Account in order to qualify for rewards, and enter on the Ultimate Doom Sweepstakes page below!

Enter the Sweepstakes

Once created, you will receive Ultimate Doom Raffle Tickets in your in-game mail to get you started! After you have signed up, log into The Contest of Champions Daily to collect your Ultimate Doom Raffle Tickets from the Ultimate Doom Sweepstakes Calendar!

Physical Prizes

  • Grand Prize: 1x Winner will receive a trip package for two (2) people (the winner and one (1) designated Guest) to attend the Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday World Premiere in Los Angeles, California (includes flights and hotel).
  • 4x Winners will receive a trip package for two (2) people (the winner and one (1) designated Guest) to a Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday Screening at El Capitan Theatre in Los Angeles, California (includes flights and hotel)

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we have to enter with our Kabam ID in order to qualify for the in-game and physical prize draws?

Yes!

In order to qualify to win any prize as a part of the Ultimate Doom Sweepstakes, you do need to enter the contest with your Kabam ID here in order to qualify.

Who is eligible to win the In-Game and Physical Prizes?

General Eligibility. To be eligible to enter, participate, and win the In-Game Prizes in the Sweepstakes, an entrant must, as of the start of the Sweepstakes Period:

  1. be a legal resident of a country or jurisdiction where (A) “Marvel Contest of Champions” (the “Game”) is lawfully published, distributed, and legally permitted to be downloaded from the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Steam (each, an “Authorized Platform”) and (B) where in-app transactions and digital purchases within the Game are fully permitted and supported by the applicable Authorized Platform in that jurisdiction (the Sweepstakes is void elsewhere and where prohibited by law); 
  2. be at least the age of majority in their place of residence or 18 years of age, whichever is older; 
  3. have downloaded the Game on their device; 
  4. create, or have created, an in-Game account (i.e. Kabam ID) and be using such account to redeem Ultimate Doom Raffle Tickets (as defined below); and 
  5. be in good standing with respect to their account, with no undisclosed violations, (collectively, “General Eligibility Criteria”).

For entrants who are legal residents of the United States, entrants must be legal residents of one of the forty-eight (48) states other than New York and Florida, or the District of Columbia, and must be physically located within one of those eligible jurisdictions at the time of entry. These Sweepstakes are void in New York, Florida, Québec, New South Wales, South Australia, Australian Capital Territory, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Italy, Mexico, Poland, and any country, territory, or jurisdiction where prohibited, restricted, or sanctioned by law, or where local regulations would require registration, bonding, localization, or translation that has not been satisfied by Kabam.

Physical Prize Eligibility. 

To be eligible to enter, participate, and win any of the Physical Prizes (defined below), an entrant must: 

  1. fully and accurately complete and submit the official online entry form available at https://playcontestofchampions.com/ultimate-doom-sweepstakes/ during the Sweepstakes Period (“Online Entry Form”); and 
  2. meet all General Eligibility Criteria as of the start of and throughout the Sweepstakes Period and be a legal resident of, and physically located within, one of the following countries:
  • Australia (excluding New South Wales, South Australia, and Australian Capital Territory)
  • Canada (excluding Québec)
  • Denmark
  • Finland
  • Germany
  • Metropolitan France
  • Netherlands
  • New Zealand
  • Singapore
  • Spain
  • Sweden
  • Switzerland
  • United Kingdom
  • United States of America (including the District of Columbia, but excluding New York, Florida, Puerto Rico, and all other U.S. Territories and possessions)
What if my Kabam ID email isn't one I have access to anymore?

As we will need to contact you via your Kabam ID for any physical prizes that are won, you will need to contact the Support team in order to change your email to one that you are able to access and use regularly.

Do the ballots stack month to month, or are they specific to one month's draw?

Yes, Raffle Tickets do stack. Players will want to continue collecting tickets throughout the whole event to increase their chances in the draws.

Do we need to do anything with the Ultimate Doom Raffle Tickets to enter?

No, you just need to collect the tickets in your Inventory. Draws will be done based on eligible players and the number of tickets in their Inventory.
