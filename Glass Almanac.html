As the eagerly anticipated HBO Max series Harry Potter edges closer to its premiere, the network is swiftly moving towards production of its second season, based on the novel Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets. To this end, three new actors have been cast for significant roles.

Arthur Weasley, Moaning Myrtle, and Colin Creevey Make Their Entrance

In the upcoming second season of HBO Max’s *Harry Potter* series, three new actors have been selected to complete the ensemble. Rafe Spall (*Trying*) will step into the shoes of Arthur Weasley, father to Ron (Alastair Stout) and Ginny (Bonnie Hill). Molly Hewitt-Richards (*Casualty*) will take on the role of the ghost Moaning Myrtle, while Jasper Ambrose (*The Detective Sheep*) will portray Colin Creevey, a new Gryffindor student excited to meet Harry Potter (Dominic McLaughlin).

In *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets*, the “boy who lived” faces a new threat at Hogwarts as the opening of the mysterious Chamber of Secrets endangers the students of the wizarding school. For Spall, the *Harry Potter* series is somewhat of a family affair, as his father, Timothy Spall, appeared in the film saga adapted from J.K. Rowling’s novels, portraying Peter Pettigrew/Wormtail.

This trio joins the already selected cast for season 2 of *Harry Potter*, which includes Kit Harington (Gilderoy Lockhart), Lenny Rush (Dobby), and Billy Barratt (Tom Riddle). The *Harry Potter* series is penned and produced by Francesca Gardiner (*Succession*), with Mark Mylod also serving as an executive producer and directing several episodes. For season 2, Gardiner shares the role of showrunner with Jon Brown (*Succession*). The first season of the series, *Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone*, is set to debut on HBO Max this coming Christmas.
